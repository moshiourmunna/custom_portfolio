Our Heritage & Story

Three decades of cotton expertise

Founded in Narayanganj, Islam Textile grew from a weaving shed into an integrated mill — spinning, weaving, and dyeing & finishing under one quality system.

The work stays on cotton woven textiles. Buyers get one accountable partner, lot identity from fiber to packed roll, and lead times they can plan around.

Our Journey

Milestones along the way

From a Narayanganj weaving shed to a mill that holds spinning, weaving, and finishing on one campus.

  1. 1990

    Founded

    Weaving shed established in Narayanganj’s textile belt.

  2. 2002

    Spinning added

    In-house ring spinning for weaving-grade cotton yarn.

  3. 2010

    Air-jet expansion

    Loom hall commissioned for export greige capacity.

  4. 2016

    Dyehouse online

    Reactive dyeing and finishing for export-ready fabric.

  5. 2024

    Global reach

    Programs for buyers across 25+ countries.
