Our quality assurance process

  1. 1. Yarn check Evenness and strength before the beam
  2. 2. Loom-side Greige defects caught on the weaving floor
  3. 3. 4-point Fabric mapped before it is packed
  4. 4. Laboratory Physical and color tests on the lot
  5. 5. Shade panel Approved against the buyer standard
  6. 6. Dispatch Rolls and documents leave together
