Mill Efficiency Upgrade Completes Phase One

Islam Textile has completed the first phase of its mill efficiency program at the Narayanganj campus, focused on loom monitoring, in-process inspection checkpoints, and clearer lot identification for woven cotton programs.

What changed

Operators now work with tighter feedback loops between weaving and finishing. That helps stabilize handfeel and construction for repeat orders — especially for greige and piece-dyed fabrics destined for export markets.

Inspection stations capture construction, shade, and finishing notes earlier in the flow, so deviations are corrected before packing and dispatch from Bangladesh.

What buyers can expect

More consistent sampling-to-bulk alignment, clearer documentation on lot status, and faster internal escalation when a construction needs correction before shipment through Chattogram port.
